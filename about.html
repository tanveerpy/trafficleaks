About Tracking Leak Detective

Our Mission

At Tracking Leak Detective, our mission is simple: to stop businesses from bleeding ad spend due to broken data and untracked funnels.

We realized that while ad agencies are great at driving traffic, they often lack the deeply technical skills required to build bulletproof tracking infrastructure. As a result, businesses were throwing money at Facebook and Google without actually knowing what was working.

What We Do

We step in as an independent, third-party auditor and technical implementation team. We don't run your ads. We don't design your website. We strictly handle data forensics, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) configuration, Google Tag Manager (GTM) deployment, and Conversion API setups.

By finding the leaks in your funnel and fixing your data foundation, we empower you to scale your business with absolute confidence.
