Common Drop-off Points I Check

I review your website to find technical issues, broken links, and poor user experiences that cost you sales.

Landing Page Bounce

Traffic arrives but does not engage. Analyzing above-the-fold friction, load time anomalies, and mismatch between ad intent and page content.

Metric Focus: Engagement Rate

Add-to-Cart Drop-off

High-intent users failing to initiate checkout. Investigating UX friction, hidden costs reveal, or broken dynamic add-to-cart scripts.

Metric Focus: Cart-to-View Ratio

Form Abandonment

Users start filling forms but quit. Tracing field-level drop-offs, validation errors, and mobile keyboard friction points.

Metric Focus: Form Completion %

Checkout Bleed

Losing users at the final step. Auditing payment gateway integrations, trust signals, and multi-step checkout flow integrity.
