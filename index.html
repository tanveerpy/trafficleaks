Common Tracking Leaks I Find

These data discrepancies drain your budget and ruin your algorithmic optimization.

Revenue Mismatch

GA4 revenue does not match sales in your CRM or backend.

Phantom Conversions

Meta reports conversions that do not exist in the backend.

Conversion Errors

Google Ads conversions are missing or duplicated.

UTM Failures

UTMs are broken, inconsistent, or stripping attribution data.

Funnel Drop-offs

Unexplained spikes in users dropping off at checkout or lead forms.

Bad Data Optimization

Paid traffic is being optimized using bad or incomplete data signals.
